# 🐾 The Story

Imagine a shelter full of pets waiting for their person.

There are pets who need homes, shelters that care for them, people
looking to adopt, and health information that needs to stay organized.

**Humane Pet Adoption Portal** brings these pieces together through a
clean and structured **RESTful backend**.
